Experimental AZJ2-only SystemUI hook. Installs disabled.
Enable the module in KernelSU GXR, then choose Soft restart in Modules.
Close and reopen an app window to see its border and resting caption pill disappear.
Point at the top of the window to reveal the pill; the caption drag area remains.
Disable the checkbox to show pills, then reopen windows to restore stock rims.
See README.md for exact steps, limits, and recovery.
